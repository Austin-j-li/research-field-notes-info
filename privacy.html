Research Field Notes

Privacy policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026.

Research Field Notes is a personal automation tool for Austin Li's research video library. It has one authorized channel owner. It does not provide account registration for others.

Google data and permissions

The tool uses Google OAuth authorization to access the owner's YouTube channel ID, video metadata, captions, and research playlists. It uses these data to upload research episodes, attach captions, prevent duplicate uploads, and organize the library.

The YouTube permission permits broader video management than these operations require. The tool restricts its operations to the designated research library. It does not use YouTube data for advertisements or sale.

Storage and service providers

Protected credentials support the automatic connection. Long-lived Google credentials stay outside the Claude cloud session in protected storage. A short-lived access token can exist in process memory during an API request.

Claude provides research and production automation. Google and YouTube store the uploaded videos, captions, and playlists. GitHub stores a private archive of episode files and metadata. ElevenLabs receives narration text to produce speech. It does not receive Google credentials.

Public research papers supply the educational material. The tool does not need access to Google email, contacts, calendars, or documents. This public information site contains no Google credentials or private library links.

Retention and revocation

The owner retains videos and episode records until the owner removes them. The owner can delete videos in YouTube Studio, remove private archive files, and revoke the app through Google Account connections. After revocation, the tool cannot refresh its authorization. The owner can remove the stored authorization from the protected environment.

Limited use

The tool's use and transfer of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The tool does not use Google user data to train a generalized AI model.

This website

This static site uses no analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process ordinary server request data under its own privacy policy.

Contact

Contact the owner through the Austin Li GitHub profile. Do not post credentials or private video links in a public issue.

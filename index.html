A PERSONAL RESEARCH LIBRARY

Research Field Notes

Research Field Notes is Austin Li's personal tool for a YouTube library of visual research explanations.

Its three subjects are financial economics, Bayesian statistics, and quantitative finance. Each full episode connects several papers through original diagrams and narrated explanations.

What the Google connection does

The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload episodes, attach captions, and add videos to research playlists. It uses the channel owner's authorization. It does not accept other users.

Episodes use unlisted visibility. Anyone with an episode or playlist link can watch it. This page does not list those links.

Data and control

Google authorization serves only the research library. Long-lived credentials stay in protected storage. The channel owner can revoke access through the Google Account permissions page.
